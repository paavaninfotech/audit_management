# Privacy Policy

The Audit Management System app does not collect or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party.

## Data Storage
All data entered into the app is stored within your ERPNext instance and remains under your control.

## Data Usage
- The app does not track users.
- No personal data is shared externally.
- The developer does not access or use your data.

## Cookies and Analytics
The app does not use cookies or any third-party analytics tools.

## Contact
For any questions or concerns regarding privacy, please reach out via your GitHub repository or contact method listed in the repository.
